Quick answers about using ViteSakuga. For deeper guides, browse the [wiki](/wiki).

## What is this site?

A community archive for animation clips and manga panels. See [What is ViteSakuga?](/wiki/about-vitesakuga) for the short version.

## Do I need an account?

No — browsing, searching, and watching are public. An account adds voting, commenting, playlists, saved searches, tag following, uploads, and edit suggestions.

## Why can’t I upload?

New accounts start as **novices** and can’t upload yet. Moderators promote eligible novices through the queue in the admin area, based on everyday useful activity. See [Roles and points](/wiki/roles-and-points).

## How do I search precisely?

Plain text works, but you can also use numeric qualifiers and tag exclusions: `width:>1000`, `likes:>10`, `video_width:=1920`, `action -movies`. The full list lives in [Search operators](/wiki/search-operators).

## How do votes work?

One like/dislike per user per post. Everything you like lands in your “Liked posts” playlist automatically.

## How do I fix a post’s metadata?

Open the post, choose **Suggest an edit**, change the fields, and check the diff. Eligible uploaders, the owner, or staff review it, and you get a notification when a decision is made. Details in [Wiki-edit suggestions](/wiki/wiki-edit-suggestions).

## Can I save a search?

Yes. Signed-in users can name the current search (text, tags, sort, and period), reapply it from the search box later, or delete it.

## How do I follow a tag?

Open the tag’s page and follow it. Posts carrying your followed tags from the last 14 days show up in the “New from followed tags” discovery view.

## How do I report a post?

Use the report action on the post detail page and include a reason. Moderators review recent reports in the admin area.

## Is my account secure?

Sign-in options include email/password (12 characters minimum, approved email providers), GitHub, Google, and passkeys. Two-factor authentication with recovery codes is available, and deleting your account anonymizes your data while public content stays attributed to “Deleted user”.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press `?` to open the shortcut list. Highlights: `Mod+K` focuses search, `G` then `P`/`U`/`S` jumps between Posts, Upload, and Settings, and video posts support frame-by-frame navigation.
