Post metadata is a wiki: instead of asking staff for changes, signed-in uploaders can propose edits that peers and owners review.

## Proposing an edit

1. Open the post and choose **Suggest an edit**.
2. Change the fields you want to improve — title, description, source, titles in other languages, episode or chapter numbers.
3. Review the field-level diff before submitting. Empty suggestions (nothing changed) are rejected automatically.

## How review works

- Eligible uploaders, the post owner, and staff can approve or reject a suggestion, following the workflow rules enforced on the server.
- Every applied edit passes through the same sanitization as a direct edit, so an approval can never smuggle content a direct edit would reject.
- Suggestions need peer approval before they are applied — a single vote is not enough to change a post.

## Tracking suggestions

The post’s edit history shows pending, applied, and rejected suggestions. When a decision is made on yours, you get an inbox notification with a link back to the post.

## What you cannot edit through suggestions

Media keys (video or thumbnail swaps) and tag relinking have their own pipelines and are intentionally out of scope for edit suggestions.
