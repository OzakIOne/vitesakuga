ViteSakuga is a community archive for animation clips and manga panels, in the spirit of Sakugabooru. Every post is uploaded, described, and reviewed by contributors, so the archive stays searchable and trustworthy.

## Two kinds of posts

- **Video posts** carry an animation clip (mp4, avi, mov, wmv, flv, or mkv, up to 200 MiB) with a generated thumbnail and optional episode metadata (season, episode, source type, source URL).
- **Image posts** carry up to ten manga panels or key frames (jpg, jpeg, png, or webp, up to 10 MiB each), ordered into a gallery. The first image becomes the post thumbnail, and volume/chapter numbers identify where a panel comes from.

## How the archive stays curated

Metadata quality is a shared job:

- Anyone signed in can [suggest a field-level edit](/wiki/wiki-edit-suggestions) to a post.
- Uploaders follow [tagging conventions](/wiki/tagging-guidelines) so searches stay predictable.
- Staff review reports and edit suggestions from the admin area.
- [Points and roles](/wiki/roles-and-points) gate who can upload and reward useful contributions.

## What you can do without an account

Browsing, searching, filtering, and watching are open to everyone. Signing in unlocks voting, commenting, playlists, saved searches, tag following, uploads, and edit suggestions.
