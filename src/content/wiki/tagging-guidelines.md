Tags are how the archive stays searchable. A post with precise tags is easy to find; a post with vague tags disappears.

## Basics

- Tags are lowercase, hyphenated tokens such as `action`, `movies`, or a series title.
- Add the series title tag on every post, plus episode or chapter context in the dedicated fields rather than as tags.
- Use existing tags when they fit — the autocomplete suggestions while typing help you match the community vocabulary.

## What makes a good tag

- **Specific over generic.** Prefer the exact character, series, or technique over a broad umbrella term when one exists.
- **Describable, not searchable.** Don’t add tags for things users can filter with [search operators](/wiki/search-operators), like resolution or score.
- **No duplicates or variants.** One spelling per concept; if two forms exist, use the established one.

## Correcting tags later

Posts are not frozen. If a post was tagged poorly, [suggest a wiki edit](/wiki/wiki-edit-suggestions) on the post detail page — tag relinking has its own pipeline, and your suggestion will route through review.

## Following tags

You can follow a tag from its page. Followed tags power the “New from followed tags” discovery view, which shows posts from the last 14 days carrying at least one tag you follow.
